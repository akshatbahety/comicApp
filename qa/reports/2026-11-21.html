BUG-001 Major F-017 (also affects F-018)

Steps: 1. Set the browser to 375 px wide (or ~1024 px desktop, where panels are about 100 px). 2. /create, Enhance off, review on, 3 panels, any idea. 3. Type a long caption (100–140 characters; the field allows 140) for panel 1. 4. Approve and draw, or open the comic's detail page.

Expected: The caption stays inside the panel (wraps or scrolls) and the panel number and Regenerate button stay usable.

Actual: The caption box grows taller than the panel: the first line is cut off above the panel and the box covers the panel number and the Regenerate button. document.elementFromPoint at the button centre returns the FIGCAPTION, so Regenerate for that panel cannot be clicked. Seen with English, Japanese and Hindi. The PNG download is fine.
